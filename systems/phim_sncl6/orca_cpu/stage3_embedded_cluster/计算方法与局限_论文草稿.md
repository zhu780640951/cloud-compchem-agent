# 计算方法与局限（论文 / SI 草稿）

> 参数全部取自实际输入文件和阶段 1 记录（`阶段1计算记录_2-苯基咪唑SnCl6_20261024.zip`），可直接据此改写。T₁ 结构上的 SOC 与辐射速率见 `阶段3_结果汇总.md` 第 4 节。

## 一、中文版（挑战杯申报书 / 答辩用）

**周期计算。** 以单晶 X 射线衍射结构为初始结构，X–H 键长先按中子衍射典型值校正。用 Quantum ESPRESSO 7.5 在固定实验晶胞下做结构优化：PBE 泛函加 D3(BJ) 色散校正，PAW 赝势（pslibrary 1.0.0），平面波截断 60/480 Ry，3×2×2 Γ 中心 k 点网格，原子受力收敛到 0.02 eV/Å 以下（最终 0.018 eV/Å）。在优化结构上计算能带和投影态密度（PDOS），PDOS 用 4×3×2 k 点网格。带隙另用 HSE06 杂化泛函校正（SG15 模守恒赝势，交换截断 120 Ry，3×2×2 k 点网格）。

**嵌入团簇模型。** 从优化后的晶体中取出 53 原子的电中性团簇：1 个 [SnCl₆]²⁻、与它氢键相连的 2 个 2-苯基咪唑鎓阳离子和 2 个水分子。7×5×3 晶胞块中其余的完整分子，全部用静电势拟合点电荷代替，共 11,077 个。点电荷按分子分别拟合（B3LYP/def2-SVP，Sn 用 def2-TZVP 及赝势），每个分子的总电荷约束为其形式电荷。

**量子化学计算**（ORCA 6.1.1）：
- **基态和激发态**：ωB97X-D3 泛函，def2-SVP 与 def2-TZVP 两套基组（Sn 用 def2 赝势），RIJCOSX 近似配 def2/J 辅助基组。激发态用 Tamm–Dancoff 近似（TDA），单重态和三重态各求 15 个。
- **自旋–轨道耦合**：标量相对论 ZORA 哈密顿量。轻元素用 ZORA-def2-TZVP，Sn 用 SARC-ZORA-TZVP，辅助基组为 SARC/J，SOC 积分用 RI-SOMF(1X)，积分格点 DefGrid3，单重态和三重态各 10 个。作为对照，单个阳离子（不含 [SnCl₆]²⁻）在气相中用相同方法计算。
- **T₁ 结构优化**：在嵌入环境中用非限制性 DFT（UKS，ωB97X-D3/def2-SVP）优化最低三重态，只放开承载 T₁ 的阳离子，其余原子固定在晶体位置。该阳离子上有 3 个氢原子离负点电荷小于 2.5 Å，也一并固定，以免它们被裸点电荷吸过去而结构塌缩。
- **激发态分析**：每个激发态的空穴与电子在各片段（两个阳离子、[SnCl₆]²⁻、水）上的分布，由 TDA 激发振幅与 Löwdin 轨道片段组成加权得到，片段间电荷转移按 IFCT 方式估算。轨道与自然跃迁轨道图由 cube 文件渲染，等值面 ±0.025 a.u.
- **发射能**：在 T₁ 结构上分别用 ΔSCF 法（T₁ 与 S₀ 的总能差）和 TDA 法计算垂直发射能。绝热能 E₀₋₀ 取 T₁ 和 S₀ 各自优化结构下的总能差，未计入零点能。

**局限。**
1. 周围晶格在计算中保持固定，没有考虑邻近分子随激发而弛豫。
2. 点电荷是裸电荷，没有泡利排斥。T₁ 优化中通过固定 3 个靠近负点电荷的 H 原子来避免由此引起的塌缩。
3. ωB97X-D3 用默认的范围分离参数，没有针对本体系调参。这类泛函对三重态激发能一般偏高约 0.1–0.3 eV。
4. 只给出垂直能量和绝热能量，没有零点能和振动展宽，计算值不能和实验峰形直接比较。
5. 系间窜越用的 SOC 矩阵元在基态结构上计算；磷光相关的 T₁ 结构上的 SOC 由第 5 批补算。
6. 从 [SnCl₆]²⁻ 三重态到阳离子 T₁ 的三重态能量转移，只能从能级顺序和态的混合成分作定性判断，没有计算转移速率。
7. 两个阳离子的三重态在 TDA 中只差 12 meV，UKS 和 TDA 给出的高低顺序相反。实际晶体中两者都可能发光。
8. 周期 PBE 计算会低估带隙（2.20 eV），只用来判断带边的轨道组成；带隙数值用 HSE06 的 2.96 eV。HSE06 只在 3×2×2 k 网格上做了单点，没有算能带。

## 二、English version (SI, Computational Details)

**Periodic calculations.** Starting from the single-crystal X-ray structure (X–H distances first normalized to typical neutron-diffraction values), atomic positions were optimized with Quantum ESPRESSO 7.5 at fixed experimental cell parameters, using the PBE functional with Grimme's D3(BJ) dispersion correction, PAW pseudopotentials (pslibrary 1.0.0), kinetic-energy cutoffs of 60/480 Ry (wavefunction/charge density) and a Γ-centred 3×2×2 k-point mesh, until residual forces were below 0.02 eV Å⁻¹. Band structures and projected densities of states (4×3×2 mesh) were computed for the optimized structure, and the band gap was corrected with the HSE06 hybrid functional (SG15 norm-conserving pseudopotentials, 120 Ry exact-exchange cutoff, 3×2×2 k-point mesh).

**Embedded-cluster model.** A charge-neutral 53-atom cluster comprising one [SnCl₆]²⁻ anion, the two hydrogen-bonded 2-phenylimidazolium cations and two water molecules was cut from the optimized crystal. All other whole molecules within a neutral 7×5×3 supercell block (11,077 sites) were represented by electrostatic-potential-fitted point charges, obtained per molecule at the B3LYP/def2-SVP level (def2-TZVP with ECP for Sn) and constrained to the formal molecular charge.

**Molecular calculations.** All cluster calculations were performed with ORCA 6.1.1. Ground- and excited-state calculations used the range-separated hybrid ωB97X-D3 with the def2-SVP and def2-TZVP basis sets (def2-ECP for Sn) and the RIJCOSX approximation (def2/J auxiliary basis). Excited states (15 singlets and 15 triplets) were obtained within the Tamm–Dancoff approximation. Spin–orbit coupling matrix elements were computed by SOC-TDA with the scalar-relativistic ZORA Hamiltonian, ZORA-def2-TZVP (H, C, N, O, Cl) and SARC-ZORA-TZVP (Sn) basis sets, the SARC/J auxiliary basis, the RI-SOMF(1X) spin–orbit operator and DefGrid3 integration grids; an isolated 2-phenylimidazolium cation was computed at the same level as a heavy-atom-free reference. The lowest triplet was optimized in the embedding environment at the UKS ωB97X-D3/def2-SVP level, relaxing only the cation carrying the triplet while keeping all other atoms at their crystal positions; three hydrogen atoms of this cation lying within 2.5 Å of negative point charges were also fixed to avoid artificial collapse onto bare point charges. Hole and electron distributions over the fragments (the two cations, [SnCl₆]²⁻ and water) were obtained for every excited state by weighting the Löwdin fragment populations of the occupied and virtual orbitals with the TDA amplitudes, and interfragment charge transfer was estimated in the IFCT manner. Vertical emission energies were evaluated both by ΔSCF and by TDA at the T₁ geometry; the adiabatic energy E₀₋₀ was taken as the difference between the T₁ and S₀ minima without zero-point correction.

**Limitations.** The environment is frozen and represented by bare point charges; ωB97X-D3 was used without range-separation tuning and typically overestimates triplet energies by ~0.1–0.3 eV; vibronic effects and zero-point energies are not included; triplet energy transfer from [SnCl₆]²⁻-centred triplets to the cation T₁ was not computed explicitly.
